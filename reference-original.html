a little letter for

my love

I made a mess of things. Here's me, trying to clean it up — one honest word at a time.

My Love,

I keep replaying it — the way I acted, the words I chose, the moment I could have just stopped and listened instead. I didn't. And I'm sorry.

You deserve someone who's patient with you even when things are messy, someone who notices when you've gone quiet and asks why instead of assuming it's fine. I want to be that person, and today I wasn't.

I'm not writing this expecting to be forgiven right away — I know that's not how it works. I just wanted you to know that I see it, I own it, and I'm going to do better. Not because I'm afraid of losing you, but because you're the person I want to keep choosing, carefully, every day.

Take your time. I'll be here.

— always yours

I'll listen first, and talk second.
I'll say it before it turns into a fight.
I'll choose "us" over being right.
I'll never make you feel small again.

So... where do we stand?

 

Thank you for hearing me out.
I love you — properly, messily, always.

made with a very sorry heart
